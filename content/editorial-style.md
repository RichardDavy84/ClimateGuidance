# Website editorial style

Richard’s review instructions of 19 October 2026 apply to future website edits.

- Use full stops, commas, colons or parentheses in place of U+2014 punctuation.
- State the subject directly and explain what it does.
- Use positive descriptions of scope and assumptions. Avoid contrastive formulations built around “X, not Y” or “we are not doing X”.
- Research pages summarise the science, contributions and collaborators. Keep consultancy distinctions out of those summaries.
- Use Climate Tools in navigation and Good Summer Index in visible copy. Keep existing routes and underlying data identifiers stable.
- Consulting has its own visible navigation tab, timescale section, practical examples and FAQs.
