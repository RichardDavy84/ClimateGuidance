What does this comparison mean?

The atmospheric comparison is an immediate change in stock. Land and oceans can release some carbon back as concentrations fall, and temperature responds over time. The regional comparison is simply an amount divided by an annual emissions rate. Both comparisons hold other conditions fixed to illustrate scale.
