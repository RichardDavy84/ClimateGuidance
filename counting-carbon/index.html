A book by Dr Richard Davy

Counting
Carbon

A Practical Guide to Carbon Budgets, Sinks and Removal

Carbon-management claims arrive in different units, over different periods and with different assumptions. This book brings them into a common frame: tonnes, rates, reservoir capacity, permanence and the resources that approaches share.

Read the bookExplore the free tools

The method

Make the claim comparable. Then test its scale.

Common units

Bring claims into tonnes of CO₂, with a clear time period and boundary.

Transparent assumptions

Keep the rates, reservoir capacities and deployment choices open to examination.

Physical constraints

Follow permanence, timescales and shared resources through to the total benefit.

Inspired by David MacKay’s Sustainable Energy: Without the Hot Air, the book applies common units, transparent assumptions and inspectable calculations to carbon management.
