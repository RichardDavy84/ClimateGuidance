Dashboard examples for climate-sensitive decisions

These examples show how Climate Guidance turns complex datasets into readable interfaces for specific decisions. Interactive Examples

Interactive climate data stories built from reanalysis, observations, and climate-model output. Each tool turns a specific climate question into something explorable.

Marine Heatwave Seasonal Forecasts

A prototype that estimates the probability of marine heatwaves above chosen category thresholds up to six months ahead. This can be customised for aquaculture operators, fish farms, insurers, and coastal planners.

Snow Forecasts for Nordic Ski Centres

A seasonal snow-depth dashboard concept for ski centres and travel operators. A paid version can include named resorts, business thresholds, custom climate metrics, and decision support for pricing, staffing, marketing, and risk planning.

Reisa National Park Climate Explorer

A protected-area planning tool that connects local climate futures with conservation priorities, scenario choices, and traffic-light risk categories for practical adaptation decisions.

Two Weeks of Warm Nights

Explore where European summer nights now remain warm for at least two weeks at a time. The map shows how sustained warm-night conditions have expanded between 1981–2000 and 2005–2024.
