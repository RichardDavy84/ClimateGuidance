The connecting thread

Ask the physical question. Then follow the numbers.

Making complex climate science quantitatively understandable and useful. Across different subjects, I return to three principles.

01

Physical scale

Is the effect large enough to matter? Put rates, quantities and timescales into a common frame.

02

Transparent assumptions

Keep the assumptions visible, distinguish evidence from scenarios, and make uncertainty inspectable.

03

Useful outputs

Turn complex information into figures, metrics and tools that someone else can question and use.

A practical guide to carbon budgets, sinks and removal.

Carbon-management claims arrive in different units, over different periods and with different assumptions. This book brings them into a common frame: tonnes, rates, reservoir capacity, permanence and the resources that approaches share.
