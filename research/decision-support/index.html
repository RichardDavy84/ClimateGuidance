Design around a question

Climate information becomes useful when it is designed around the question someone needs to answer. A park manager, a researcher and a reader examining a carbon claim may need very different quantities from the same underlying climate evidence.

From reanalysis to local information

KrigR, developed with Erik Kusch, supports downloading and statistically downscaling ERA5 reanalysis. The distinction between more grid cells and more information is essential: a high-resolution result still depends on covariates, data quality and the assumptions of the method.

Make the route from data to answer visible

The protected-area explorers retain model cases, time windows and definitions alongside maps and time series. The snow and marine interfaces show ensemble information; their archive periods and prototype status are shown alongside the results.

Credit the work behind the interface

The Reisa and Pasvik tools carry CLAP / Interreg Aurora attribution. The gallery links each interface to its method and scope so visitors can judge what it can support.
