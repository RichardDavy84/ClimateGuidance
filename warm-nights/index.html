How to read this

For each grid cell, each summer is counted as a “yes” if it contains at least one 14-day period where the daily minimum 2 m temperature stayed above the selected threshold every day. The period maps show the percentage of summers with such a spell. The change map shows 2005–2024 minus 1981–2000 in percentage points.
